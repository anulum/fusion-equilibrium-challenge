# Experiment ledger

One row per experiment or measurement of the entry, from 28 September 2026: what was asked, how it was measured,
the number, the decision. Negative results are kept on purpose. Work before that date is summarised in section 0.

How to read it:

- A protocol named with a short hexadecimal prefix is the SHA-256 of the file that fixed the question, the
  measurement and the decision rule before the first result existed.
- "The first agent" and "the second agent" are two agents of Arcane Sapience, the entrant's research system; they
  reviewed each other's work. "The entrant" is the person who directs it and decides.
- "ML350" is the laboratory's two-socket Xeon E5-2640 server; "Kaggle" is Kaggle's free notebook tier.
- A number after `#` is a Codabench submission. 1 µS is 10⁻⁶ of the composite score.
- Cohorts of whole shots are held out of every fit they judge. Where a cohort had been used before, the row says so.

Score: S = 0.55 R²ψ + 0.15 R²{q95,βN} + 0.10 (1 − D_LCFS) + 0.20 Consistency (7 derived scalars).
